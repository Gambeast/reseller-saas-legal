# Reseller SaaS Privacy Policy

**Project:** Reseller SaaS is an independently operated private development project. It is not a registered company or a publicly available subscription service.  
**Person responsible for personal-information protection:** Reseller SaaS project operator  
**Contact:** [resellersaas.canada@hotmail.com](mailto:resellersaas.canada@hotmail.com)  
**Publication, effective and last-updated date:** November 16, 2026

## Scope

This policy describes the current limited-access inventory and eBay listing application, including the operator's live seller workflow and separate eBay Sandbox testing. Sandbox tests are intended to use synthetic inventory and photos, not real buyer information. The policy will be reviewed before the project offers access to other sellers or materially changes its data practices.

## Information handled and purposes

The operator and permitted testers enter inventory and business records such as Lots, individual physical items, item descriptions, photos, listing drafts, Listings and Sales. Reseller SaaS uses them to prepare and manage listings, preserve original Lot ownership, avoid duplicate publication or sales, and reconcile business history.

When a seller authorizes eBay access, Reseller SaaS receives the seller-account identity, authorized permissions and OAuth credentials. It sends listing details and photos to eBay to verify, create or observe listings. It receives listing, order and transaction facts such as Item IDs, order and line IDs, quantities, payment, cancellation, refund and amount information to match sales to physical inventory. Some identifiers and transaction facts may be personal information even without a buyer name. Normalized order observations are designed to exclude buyer names and shipping addresses.

The application also handles information needed to run and protect the service: essential OAuth state and Mobile Photo session cookies, Mobile Photo device/session records, photos and revisions, request timing, limited error diagnostics and audit/history events. The operator receives information included in emails sent to the privacy contact. Connecting eBay and using Mobile Photo are optional, but their functions are unavailable without the corresponding authorization or session. The current application does not integrate advertising cookies or third-party marketing analytics.

## Access, sharing and location

Access to the private application is limited to the operator and people permitted to use it. Reseller SaaS sends information to eBay only for authorized marketplace workflows. The operator does not sell eBay or other personal information.

Service providers can process limited information needed for transport and communication: the live application's HTTPS ingress provider (currently ngrok), Microsoft for privacy-contact email, and GitHub Pages for this policy page. GitHub Pages may record a visitor's IP address for security. If a supervised Sandbox seller authorization uses a temporary Cloudflare Quick Tunnel, Cloudflare relays the short-lived OAuth redirect, including its code and state; the local callback helper does not retain them. These providers may process information outside Québec or Canada. Application business records are maintained in the operator-controlled database, rather than in the public policy site.

## Security, cookies and retention

Production eBay refresh credentials are encrypted in the application database. The separate TEST Sandbox broker stores its seller credential in an access-controlled operator file. Access and publication actions have application guards; no storage or transmission method is risk-free. Essential session and OAuth cookies support the requested features; refusing them may prevent those features from working.

Inventory, Listing, Sale, order and audit records are retained while needed for operation, reconciliation, accounting or applicable obligations. Revoking eBay access does not automatically erase those records. The project does not yet have a fixed retention schedule or self-service deletion/export controls. Requests are reviewed manually against applicable law, eBay requirements and the need to preserve legitimate business records. The operator does not claim that eBay account-deletion notifications automatically erase all related application records.

## Your requests and complaints

Email [resellersaas.canada@hotmail.com](mailto:resellersaas.canada@hotmail.com) to ask about personal information held about you, request access or correction, ask for deletion or export, remove an eBay connection, or make a privacy complaint. The operator may request the minimum information needed to verify that the records concern you. The operator will acknowledge the request and provide a substantive response within 30 calendar days, including an explanation if a request cannot be fully granted. Do not send passwords, OAuth codes or tokens by email. You may also revoke Reseller SaaS authorization through your eBay account. If you are dissatisfied with the response, you may contact the applicable privacy regulator, including the [Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/) or the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/), as applicable.

## Changes

The published page will show its effective and last-updated dates. The operator will update it when material practices change.
